Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan 2022 | Master Forum and booth preview

Good Future and Inventec Appliances previewed their joint exhibit and Master Forum program for Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan 2022.

All ResourcesNewsInsightsVideos

Good Future and Inventec Appliances participated in Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan from December 1 to 4, 2022, at booth J318.

The exhibit introduced Chiline CATCH, Family Pal, and TTSmart, while the Master Forum brought together speakers to discuss smart healthcare and clinical applications.

The program was designed for healthcare professionals, industry partners, and visitors interested in learning more about the technologies.
