Good Future joins Min-Sheng Medical Group's 47th anniversary event

Good Future and Inventec Appliances attended the 47th anniversary of Min-Sheng Medical Group as healthcare technology partners.

All ResourcesNewsInsightsVideos

Good Future and Inventec Appliances were invited to Min-Sheng Medical Group's 47th anniversary event at Min-Sheng Smart Medical City in Taoyuan.

The event introduced the medical group's plans for smart care, digital workflows, and integrated health services. Good Future presented Chiline CATCH, while Inventec Appliances introduced Family Pal.
