Good Future showcases Chiline CATCH at Medical Fair Asia 2024

Good Future and Inventec Appliances presented Chiline CATCH, Family Pal, and TTSmart at Medical Fair Asia 2024 in Singapore.

All ResourcesNewsInsightsVideos

Good Future and Inventec Appliances participated in Medical Fair Asia 2024 at Marina Bay Sands in Singapore.

The teams presented Chiline CATCH alongside the Family Pal health-management platform and TTSmart infusion solution. Demonstrations gave visitors a closer look at the products and their intended workflows.

The event also provided an opportunity to meet distributors, healthcare professionals, and potential partners from across the region.
