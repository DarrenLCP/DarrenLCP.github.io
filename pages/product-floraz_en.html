Background

What is the gut-brain axis?

The gut and brain communicate through neural, immune, and metabolic pathways. Researchers are studying how gut microorganisms and their metabolites may relate to stress and sleep, but findings cannot be generalized across all strains or products.

When evaluating a probiotic for bedtime use, consider the exact strain, serving size, supporting evidence, and whether the product is appropriate for your health status.

Ingredient Background

Why are GABA, short-chain fatty acids, and tryptophan discussed?

  • Short-chain fatty acids are microbial metabolites being studied in gut-brain communication.
  • Tryptophan is a dietary amino acid used by the body in serotonin synthesis.
  • GABA is an inhibitory neurotransmitter and is included here as a fermented ingredient.
Suggested Use

Designed for adults who want to add a probiotic formula to a consistent bedtime routine.

  • Take one sachet for general use or two sachets as directed on the product label.
  • Mix with warm water not exceeding 40°C and take one hour before bedtime.
  • Consult a healthcare professional before combining this product with sleep aids or prescription medication.
Precautions

Persistent sleep problems may have multiple causes and should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

  • This product is a dietary supplement and is not a treatment for insomnia, anxiety, or other medical conditions.
  • People with hypertension or heart, liver, or kidney disease should follow their physician’s advice before use.
  • Maintain regular sleep habits and review the complete product label before use.
