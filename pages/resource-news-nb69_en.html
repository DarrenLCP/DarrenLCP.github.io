Good Future at the 2025 Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan

Good Future concluded four days of discussions and demonstrations at the 2025 Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan, welcoming healthcare professionals and visitors from Taiwan and Southeast Asia.

All ResourcesNewsInsightsVideos

Good Future participated in the 2025 Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan, where the team presented its circulating tumor cell testing technology and discussed potential applications with healthcare professionals and industry partners.

Visitors from Taiwan and several Southeast Asian markets met with the team during the four-day event. The conversations focused on laboratory workflows, clinical collaboration, and opportunities for regional partnerships.

We thank everyone who visited the booth and shared their questions and perspectives with us.
