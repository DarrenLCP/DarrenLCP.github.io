About Good Future

Expanding precision medicine capabilities through CTC testing

Good Future was established in 2017. Starting from circulating tumor cell testing technology, the company has gradually built precision medicine laboratory operations, testing workflows, and collaboration capabilities based on stable testing quality and clinical experience.

Its in-house laboratory is currently responsible for blood-sample processing, image analysis, and reporting, while Good Future continues to integrate automated enrichment systems, reagents, consumables, and testing workflows to improve service availability and consistency.

Past milestones, certificates, and awards demonstrate Good Future’s significant recent progress in technology licensing, collaborative development, corporate projects, laboratory accreditation, and overseas expansion.

Milestones

Key Milestones from 2017 to 2025

These milestones, certifications, and awards trace Good Future BioMed’s development from technology licensing and laboratory operations to clinical collaboration and international expansion.

2017

Company establishment and technology licensing

  • Good Future was officially established, focusing on the commercialization of CTC negative selection technology.
  • Obtained CTC technology licensing from Chang Gung University, laying the foundation for the company’s core technology.
  • Worked with Inventec Appliances to develop a fully automated CTC detection system integrating cell enrichment, immunofluorescence staining, image acquisition, and AI-assisted interpretation.
  • Building on licensed academic technology, Good Future developed a CTC testing platform designed for standardized, automated clinical workflows.
2019

Implementing CTC testing in medical-center workflows

  • Worked with the Department of Laboratory Medicine at Taipei Veterans General Hospital to introduce the IronMag® fully automated CTC detection system.
  • Built standardized and automated CTC testing workflows informed by clinical laboratory practice.
  • Advanced collaboration with healthcare institutions, regulatory preparation, and commercial deployment of the CTC testing platform.
2020

Corporate health screening and innovation recognition

  • Signed a thousand-person circulating tumor cell (CTC) testing service project with Inventec Group, bringing CTC testing into enterprise health management.
  • Built operational experience in specimen handling and service delivery through a large-scale corporate testing project.
  • The IronMag® automated CTC detection system received the 17th National Innovation Award, demonstrating Good Future’s capability to translate innovative technology into an automated testing platform.
2023

Extending CTC testing through insurance and wellness programs

  • Partnered with Global Life, Nan Shan Life, and other insurers to include CTC testing in policyholder wellness programs and corporate health services.
  • Focused on cancer-risk assessment and health follow-up to help partners develop more differentiated health-management services.
  • Connected insurance, corporate, and health-screening channels to make CTC testing more accessible within preventive-health programs.
2024

Expanding into Southeast Asian markets

  • Began market-development work for CTC testing in Malaysia, Thailand, and other international markets.
  • As part of medical product-line brand integration, the original IronMag® CTC AutoProcessor was renamed Chiline CATCH® CTC Enrichment System, while the core technology and application workflow remained consistent.
  • Advanced regulatory assessments, ISO 13485 preparation, product registration, import/export planning, and local partner development.
  • Continued building relationships with overseas distributors, medical institutions, and testing partners to support adoption of the Chiline CATCH® CTC platform.
2025

Certifications and registrations supporting international expansion

  • Received the 2025 National Innovation Advancement Award in recognition of continued progress in CTC technology, automated workflows, and clinical implementation.
  • Extended the laboratory's ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation scope to include epithelial and mesenchymal CTC testing.
  • Established an ISO 13485:2016-certified quality management system covering relevant product development, manufacturing, and quality-control processes.
  • Obtained a Malaysian Medical Device Registration Certificate, establishing a regulatory basis for introducing the CTC testing platform in Malaysia.
