Good Future signs CTC testing service agreement with Min-An Clinic

Good Future and Min-An Traditional Chinese Medicine Clinic signed an agreement to make the Chiline CATCH CTC testing service available through the clinic.

All ResourcesNewsInsightsVideos

Good Future signed a service agreement with Min-An Traditional Chinese Medicine Clinic in Taichung.

Under the agreement, the clinic may coordinate access to the Chiline CATCH CTC testing service and provide clients with information about specimen collection and follow-up consultation.

CTC testing is an adjunct laboratory service and does not replace standard diagnostic examinations or physician assessment.
