Probiotic Strains

Five Lactobacillus strains in the FloraC™ formula

  • Lactobacillus reuteri TE-33: A strain originally isolated from the infant intestinal tract.
  • Lactobacillus rhamnosus CT-53: A strain originally isolated from the healthy female reproductive tract.
  • Lactobacillus rhamnosus F-1: A second L. rhamnosus strain originally isolated from the healthy female reproductive tract.
  • Lactobacillus salivarius AP-32: A strain studied in oral and intestinal microbiota research.
  • Lactobacillus crispatus KT-11: A heat-treated strain belonging to a species commonly found in the healthy vaginal microbiota.

What should I check before use?

FloraC™ is intended for adults looking for a capsule formula that combines Lactobacillus cultures with selected nutrients for intimate and everyday wellness support.
