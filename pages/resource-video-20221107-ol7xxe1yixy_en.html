Famous Doctor On Call | Dr. Tseng Chih-Jen on liquid biopsy

Dr. Tseng Chih-Jen discusses liquid biopsy, CTC testing, and how blood-based information may complement established clinical examinations.

All ResourcesNewsInsightsVideos

Dr. Tseng Chih-Jen of Chung Shan Medical University Hospital joins Famous Doctor On Call to discuss liquid biopsy.

The program introduces circulating tumor cells, specimen processing, and the potential role of blood-based information in cancer-related assessment and follow-up.

Liquid biopsy is an adjunct approach and does not replace pathology, imaging, or diagnosis by a qualified physician.
