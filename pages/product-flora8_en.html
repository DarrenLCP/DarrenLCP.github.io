Background

Why does age matter when choosing a probiotic?

The gut microbiota changes with age, diet, medication use, and lifestyle. Children may also require smaller serving sizes than adults, which is why the product label provides age-specific directions.

Flora8™ combines four Bifidobacterium and four Lactobacillus cultures in a family-oriented formula. It is intended for daily probiotic supplementation, not for the treatment of disease.

Product Highlights

Eight strains with age-based serving guidance

  • The formula includes eight probiotic cultures commonly used in products for women and children.

Selected bacterial strains

The eight cultures included in Flora8™

Bifidobacterium Cultures

Species commonly identified in the human intestinal microbiota

  • Bifidobacterium longum subsp. infantis: A Bifidobacterium associated with the early-life intestinal microbiota.
  • Bifidobacterium breve: A species commonly identified in the intestinal microbiota of breastfed infants.
  • Bifidobacterium bifidum: A Bifidobacterium species commonly found in the human intestine.
  • Bifidobacterium animalis subsp. lactis: A culture frequently selected for acid and bile tolerance.
Lactobacillus Cultures

Cultures commonly used in probiotic foods and supplements

  • Lactobacillus paracasei: A culture commonly included in probiotic foods and supplements.
  • Lactobacillus rhamnosus: A species frequently selected for acid and bile tolerance.
  • Lactobacillus acidophilus: A commonly used culture associated with lactose fermentation.
  • Lactobacillus plantarum: A food-associated species selected for tolerance of gastrointestinal conditions.
Suggested Use

Use the age-based serving directions provided on the product label.

  • Adults and children aged 7 or older: take 1–2 sachets with breakfast in warm water not exceeding 40°C.
  • Children aged 4–6: one sachet with breakfast. Children aged 1–3: half a sachet with breakfast.
  • For infants under one year of age, use only under the guidance of a physician.
Precautions

Check age, health status, and professional guidance before use

  • Consult a healthcare professional before use if the intended user has a medical condition or is taking medication.
  • Suitable for lacto-ovo vegetarians. Review the product label for current testing and allergen information.
  • Follow the labeled serving size and monitor tolerance when introducing any new supplement.
