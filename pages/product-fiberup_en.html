Formula Overview

1.64 g of dietary fiber per sachet with red quinoa, prebiotics, and digestive enzymes

Each 5 g sachet combines red quinoa and indigestible maltodextrin as fiber sources with isomaltooligosaccharides, inulin, galactooligosaccharides, and digestive enzymes. It contains no live probiotic cultures and may be used alone or alongside a probiotic product as directed.

Product Highlights

A practical blend of dietary fiber, prebiotics, and digestive enzymes

  • Each 5 g sachet provides 1.64 g of dietary fiber, with red quinoa as a key plant-based ingredient.
  • Indigestible maltodextrin provides soluble dietary fiber that is not readily digested in the small intestine.
  • Isomaltooligosaccharides, inulin, and galactooligosaccharides provide prebiotic substrates for gut microorganisms.
  • The enzyme blend includes amylase, lipase, protease, lactase, cellulase, bromelain, and papain.
How the Ingredients Work Together

Fiber, prebiotics, and enzymes in one daily formula

  • Red quinoa: A gluten-free plant ingredient that contributes dietary fiber and micronutrients.
  • Indigestible maltodextrin: A soluble dietary fiber that reaches the lower digestive tract largely undigested.
  • Digestive enzyme blend: Includes protease, lipase, amylase, lactase, cellulase, bromelain, and papain.
  • Rice bran concentrate and citric acid complete the powdered formula.
Formula Details

Key fiber, prebiotic, and enzyme ingredients

  • Red quinoa powder: A gluten-free source of dietary fiber with naturally occurring minerals.
  • Indigestible maltodextrin: A water-soluble dietary fiber used to increase daily fiber intake.
  • Isomaltooligosaccharides, inulin, and galactooligosaccharides: Prebiotic ingredients.
  • Digestive enzyme blend: Formulated to help break down major food components.

FAQ

Who May Consider FiberUp grade™?

FiberUp grade™ supplies dietary fiber, prebiotics, and digestive enzymes; it does not contain live probiotic cultures. The formula can complement, rather than replace, a probiotic product.
Follow the serving directions on the product label. The formula contains milk and soy ingredients, so review the allergen information before use.
