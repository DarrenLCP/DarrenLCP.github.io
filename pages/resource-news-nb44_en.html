Media Coverage | Inventec expands precision-health services with Nan Shan Life

Media coverage examined a health-promotion insurance program that connected Nan Shan Life members with selected healthcare services, including Good Future's CTC testing.

This page summarizes media coverage of a collaboration involving Inventec Group, Nan Shan Life, and healthcare service providers.

The report described how selected health-management services, including Good Future's CTC testing, were incorporated into a wellness-linked insurance program.

Eligibility, benefits, and service details are determined by the insurer and participating providers. CTC testing is an adjunct service and should not be interpreted as a stand-alone cancer diagnosis.
