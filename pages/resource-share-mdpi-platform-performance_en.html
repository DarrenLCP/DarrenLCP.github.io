Breast cancer CTC paper summary: what the automated negative selection platform revealed

See why CSV+ CTCs stood out in this study and what the results suggest for metastatic-risk stratification and recurrence monitoring.

All Resources News Insights Videos

This paper focused on CSV+ circulating tumor cells in breast cancer and used Good Future’s automated negative selection platform to enrich and evaluate them.

The study found that CSV+ CTCs, rather than EpCAM+ CTCs, were associated with higher metastatic risk.

More than 4.5 CSV+ CTCs per 2 mL of blood showed strong specificity for identifying higher-risk patients.

CSV+ CTCs also outperformed CA 15-3, CA 125, and CEA, and the combined use improved risk stratification further.

The paper reinforces the value of negative selection in preserving clinically meaningful CTC phenotypes.
