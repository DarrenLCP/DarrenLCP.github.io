Directions and Storage

Practical product information

Net content
60 g per bottle: 120 capsules, 500 mg each
Suggested use for adults
For general use, take two capsules with warm water after breakfast. Follow the product label before using a higher serving amount.
Pregnancy and use by children
Use only under the guidance of a healthcare professional.
Storage
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
Allergen information
Contains milk, soy, and ingredients derived from them. Do not use if you are allergic to these ingredients.
Warning

Turmeric-related interactions and groups that need professional advice

  • Do not use before or after surgery, during active bleeding, or while taking anticoagulant or antiplatelet medication unless directed by a physician.
  • People taking glucose-lowering medication should consult a physician before use.
  • People taking transplant anti-rejection medication should not use this product without medical guidance.
  • Do not use during pregnancy or breastfeeding unless directed by a physician. People with gallstones, liver disease, diabetes, hypertension, or multiple medications should seek professional advice before use.
